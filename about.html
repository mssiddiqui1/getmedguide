About GetMedGuide

Practical, no-fluff study materials for medical coding certification and healthcare credentials — written by someone actually going through the process.

GetMedGuide started with a focused set of guides and adds new material every month. Every guide is built around what's actually tested — not padded with filler — and every paid guide includes a free preview so you know what you're getting before you buy.

Sana Ahmed

Medical Billing & Coding Specialist · CPC Candidate (AAPC)

Sana created GetMedGuide's study materials while completing the University of Houston's 370-hour Medical Billing and Coding program and preparing for her own AAPC Certified Professional Coder (CPC) exam. Her training covers ICD-10-CM, CPT, HCPCS Level II, and ICD-10-PCS code sets, HIPAA-compliant claims, and the Codify (AAPC) encoder — and she's actively exploring how AI-assisted coding tools are reshaping the field.
