Sample cards

Four real cards from the deck, front and back, so you know exactly what you're getting before you buy:

Risk Adjustment Fundamentals #7 · Q

What is a 'risk pool'?

Risk Adjustment Fundamentals #7 · A

A group of enrollees whose collective health risk and costs are used to calculate premiums or capitated payments.

ICD-10-CM Coding for Risk Adjustment #41 · Q

Can a 'history of' code be used to report an active, ongoing chronic condition for risk adjustment purposes?

ICD-10-CM Coding for Risk Adjustment #41 · A

No. History codes indicate a resolved condition and generally do not map to HCCs representing active chronic disease management.

CMS-HCC vs. HHS-HCC Models #75 · Q

What does 'concurrent' risk adjustment mean, as used in the HHS-HCC model?

CMS-HCC vs. HHS-HCC Models #75 · A

Diagnoses from the current benefit year are used to adjust payment for that same year, rather than predicting the following year as in a prospective model.

Common Risk-Adjusted Conditions #111 · Q

What vascular condition affecting circulation, especially in the legs, is often an underreported risk-adjusted diagnosis?

Common Risk-Adjusted Conditions #111 · A

Peripheral vascular disease (peripheral artery disease).
