CPC Cheat Sheet

A one-glance reference for the code ranges, modifiers, and rules most likely to trip you up on exam day.

Exam at a glance

  • 100 multiple-choice questions (including ~10 case studies)
  • 4 hours, one session → roughly 2 min 24 sec per question
  • Open book — your printed CPT, ICD-10-CM & HCPCS manuals
  • Pass mark: 70% · open book ≠ easy — speed of lookup is the real skill

The three code sets

SetCovers
ICD-10-CMDiagnoses — the "why." 3–7 characters.
CPTProcedures/services — the "what." 5 digits, maintained by the AMA.
HCPCS Level IISupplies, drugs, DME & services not in CPT. Letter + 4 digits.

ICD-10-CM guideline reminders

Always start in the Alphabetic Index, then verify in the Tabular List — never code from the Index alone. The 7th character A/D/S marks initial, subsequent, or sequela encounters...
