Sample cards

Four real cards from the deck, front and back, so you know exactly what you're getting before you buy:

Outpatient Facility Coding Fundamentals #7 · Q

What is the difference between facility coding and professional (physician) coding for the same encounter?

Outpatient Facility Coding Fundamentals #7 · A

Facility coding reflects the resources and overhead used by the facility, while professional coding reflects the physician's cognitive and procedural work.

Ambulatory Surgery Center (ASC) Coding #38 · Q

What payment system does Medicare use to reimburse ASCs?

Ambulatory Surgery Center (ASC) Coding #38 · A

The ASC Payment System, a methodology related to but distinct from OPPS.

Observation Services #69 · Q

What is a composite APC payment as it relates to observation services?

Observation Services #69 · A

A single payment made for extended assessment and management encounters that include observation services along with qualifying visits, under specific OPPS criteria.

NCCI & OCE #103 · Q

What is the Outpatient Code Editor (OCE)?

NCCI & OCE #103 · A

A CMS software tool that evaluates outpatient facility claims for coding and billing errors and determines APC assignment.
