HCPCS Level II Quick-Reference Tree

A visual map of the HCPCS Level II code ranges — supplies, drugs, DME, and services outside CPT.

HCPCS Level II codes are one letter followed by four digits, grouped by their leading letter. They cover items and services outside CPT — drugs, supplies, durable medical equipment, and more.

LetterCoversRange
ATransportation, medical/surgical supplies, misc.A0021–A9999
BEnteral & parenteral therapyB4034–B9999
COutpatient PPS (hospital OPPS)C1052–C9999
EDurable medical equipment (DME)E0100–E8002
GProcedures / professional services (temporary)G0008–G9999
HBehavioral health & substance abuseH0001–H2037
JDrugs (non-oral) & chemotherapyJ0120–J9999
KDME & supplies (temporary)K0000–K9999
LOrthotics & prostheticsL0112–L9999
MMedical services & quality measuresM0075–M1999
PPathology & laboratoryP2028–P9615
QTemporary codesQ0035–Q9999
RDiagnostic radiology servicesR0070–R0076
STemporary national codes (private payers)S0012–S9999
TState Medicaid agency codesT1000–T5999
VVision, hearing & speech-languageV2020–V5364

Note: dental services use the separate CDT (Current Dental Terminology) code set, maintained by the ADA — not HCPCS Level II. Ranges above are approximate and update annually — always confirm in your current-year HCPCS Level II manual.
