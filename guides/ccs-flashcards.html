Sample cards

Four real cards from the deck, front and back, so you know exactly what you're getting before you buy:

Advanced ICD-10-CM Coding #7 · Q

How are 7th character extensions used in ICD-10-CM?

Advanced ICD-10-CM Coding #7 · A

They convey information such as episode of care (initial, subsequent, sequela), must occupy the 7th character position, and require placeholder 'X' to fill any empty characters before them.

Inpatient DRG Assignment & Reimbursement #41 · Q

What is the principal diagnosis?

Inpatient DRG Assignment & Reimbursement #41 · A

The condition established after study to be chiefly responsible for occasioning the admission of the patient to the hospital.

Pharmacology & Its Relationship to Coding #75 · Q

Why can't a coder assign a diagnosis code based solely on a patient's medication list?

Pharmacology & Its Relationship to Coding #75 · A

Coding conventions require that a diagnosis be documented by the provider; medication clues may prompt a query but cannot substitute for a documented diagnosis.

Healthcare Statistics & Reporting #111 · Q

What is the net autopsy rate?

Healthcare Statistics & Reporting #111 · A

Hospital autopsies performed on inpatient deaths divided by total inpatient deaths, minus unautopsied coroners' or medical examiners' cases.
