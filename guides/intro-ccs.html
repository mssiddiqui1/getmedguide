Introduction to CCS

What the advanced, hospital-wide Certified Coding Specialist credential covers, and how it compares to CCA, COC, and CIC.

What CCS is

The Certified Coding Specialist (CCS), from AHIMA, is an advanced, hospital-wide coding credential — it proves mastery across both inpatient and outpatient facility coding, rather than specializing in just one side the way COC or CIC do.

CCS is an advanced, hospital-wide credential — it doesn't split into an inpatient-only or outpatient-only track.

Who it's for

CCS is generally pursued by coders who already have real coding experience (AHIMA recommends around two years) and want a credential that signals broad, advanced hospital coding competency — often a step up from CCA.

Study tips

  • Because CCS spans both settings, don't neglect ICD-10-PCS just because you're more comfortable with CPT (or vice versa)
  • Practice full case scenarios, not just isolated code lookups — CCS leans heavily on applying guidelines to real documentation
  • Know both DRG and APC logic well enough to explain why a case groups the way it does
