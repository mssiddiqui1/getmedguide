Free LinkedIn Banners

Studying for a certification (or already hold one)? Download a free banner for your LinkedIn profile — it's a nice way to mark the milestone, and it credits GetMedGuide.com so other coders studying the same exam can find these resources too.

How to use: download the image, then on LinkedIn go to your profile → click the camera/pencil icon on your cover photo → upload. LinkedIn banners are 1584×396px — your profile photo sits in the bottom-left corner, so if it feels crowded you can reposition or crop after uploading, right in LinkedIn's own editor.

Note: these are original graphics made by GetMedGuide.com to celebrate your study progress — they are not official materials and aren't issued or endorsed by AAPC, AHIMA, or NHA.
