Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about buying, reading, and what's coming next.

How do I get a paid guide?

Click "Preview" on any paid guide to read a sample right on the site. When you're ready, click "Buy" — you'll be taken to a secure Payhip checkout page. Once payment is complete, you'll get access to the full guide.

Are the free guides really free?

Yes. Free guides are full guide pages right here on the site — no account, email, or payment required.

Do I have to download a PDF to read a guide?

No. Guides live on the site as regular pages, so you can read them, bookmark them, and share the link directly with a study partner. The one exception is the CPC flashcards, which stay as a printable PDF since they're meant to be printed and flipped through.

How often is new material added?

We're adding new guides regularly over the coming months — the Guides page updates automatically as new material is published.

Do you have video lessons?

Videos are on the way — check the Videos page, which will fill in automatically once they're published.
